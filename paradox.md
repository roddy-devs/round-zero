ROCKET
(Grim)
No Score Streaks
Reach Round 20
In Normal Nuketown, brain rot a Rad-Hound and pet it.
Call in a DAWG Killstreak in Destroyed Nuketown, put it in Sentry Mode and pet it.
Travel to Normal Nuketown and interact with the green portal in Green House Upstairs.
Survive 4 waves while enemies only take damages from their elemental weakness.
Shadow Rift for Toxic Zombies and Rad-Hounds
Light Mend for Doppelghasts
Brain Rot for Shock Mimics
SUMMONING KEY
(Sinister)
Zombies explode on death dealing damage to nearby players
Activate Cursed Mode Tier I
Throw a grenade inside the broken outside chimney of Destroyed Green House near Cul-De-Sac. This will drop the Dog Collar of Notso.
Return to Normal Nuketown and turn a Rad-Hound using Brain Rot/Psych Grenade. If done correctly, the dog will start digging in Yellow House Backyard. The dog will then stop and you can pick up the ball in its mouth.
Reach Round 40 and down yourself near the fences at the very back of Green House Backyard, behind the Perk location. If done correctly, a headstone will rise from the ground. Interact with the headstone to place the ball and dog collar.
Return to Normal Nuketown and interact with the yellow portal against the wall of Yellow House Backyard.
Survive 5 waves while killing all the enemies of each wave before the time runs out (90 seconds).
MANGLER HELMET
(Wicked)
No Arsenal

Activate Cursed Mode Tier II. Do not pick the Seed Relic for this.
Equip the Mister Peeks Field Upgrade and use the Mystery Box until Mister Peeks apppears. Activate the Field Upgrade to drop Mister Peeks on the ground and pick it up. This needs to be done in both version of Nuketown.
Place Mister Peeks on the mailbox of Yellow House in both version of Nuketown.
Reach Round 60
Pick the Ultra Knife found in the mailbox of either version Nuketown which will now be opened. Doing so will spawn a HVT Mimic. Kill the Mimic with the Knife, which can be Pack-a-Punched. Note: If you kill the Mimic with something else, it will eventually respawn the next round.
If done correctly, travel to Destroyed Nuketown and interact with the red portal that spawned in front of the Green House front balcony.
Survive 6 waves with all Armor Plates removed and with 50 less health point.
