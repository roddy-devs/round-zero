Rex Infernus Main Easter Egg (Step-by-Step)

This is a cleaned and organized walkthrough based on the original notes.

## Initial Setup

1. Spawn in and go through the first door
2. Wait for the phone to ring and answer it
3. A teleporter will appear in front of you
4. Before teleporting, look up and shoot down the basketball
5. Take the teleporter and place the world seed in the center of the pedestal

## Dread Skulls & Flames

- Two dread skulls become available
- Shoot each skull and follow it to its temple
- Keep shooting it as you're able
- When destroyed, your screen flashes white and you receive the flame
- Two braziers appear (marked on screen) where you throw the flame
- Repeat for the second dread skull

## Activate Pack-a-Punch

- Turn the dials (one on each side = 4 total)
- Turn all four to activate Pack-a-Punch

## Caltheris's Temple

1. Enter temple and look to your left
2. Four pressure plates on ground (first one risen)
3. Stand on a plate WITHOUT moving off it
4. Shoot four targets
5. Second plate rises; repeat until all four plates complete
6. Open compartment to find lantern
7. Interact with lantern → orbs spawn around map

## Dravakar's Temple - Get Fracture and open the Forge

1. Enter and turn around to look up
2. Alcove above entrance (grapple or wall jump)
3. Grapple is faster
4. Find crack in wall (right side, facing entrance)
5. Throw grenade/PHD Flop at crack
6. Collect Nyxara's Fracture
7. Go to next door in temple
8. Blue writing on wall = dial instructions
9. Interact and follow the solver for how many times to turn switches
10. Use solver tool for exact sequence

## Nyxara's Temple - Solve Switches
1. Open both doors
2. Turn right (facing the eye at back of room)
3. Dial above rubble (use Void Gun to adjust)

**Dial Configuration:**
- Dial 1: Middle
- Dial 2: Pointing right
- Dial 3: Pointing left
- Dial 4: Pointing right
- Dial 5: Middle (place Fracture from Dravakar here)
- Dial 6: Middle
- Dial 7: Pointing left

*Note: Wall jump to dial 5 to place fracture without Void Gun*

## Nexus Forge & Flames

1. Use Void Gun to pull orb into Nexus Forge center
2. Orb spirals around forge
3. Turn dial/turn forge
4. Purple flames fill braziers near Nexus fracture

## The Eye Shot

1. Take flame from brazier
2. Go to Nixara's Sanctuary (back of room with eye)
3. Shoot eye with flame
4. Use Grapple Gun or Void Gun to get there quickly
5. Walking is possible but tight time window

## Laser Door & Spider Room

1. With correct dial alignment and Fracture placed, laser door opens
2. Enter newly opened room
3. Walk to orb → orb shocks you and drops you down
4. Kill spiders
5. Walk toward tree

## Soulbox Step (YOU are the soulbox)

- Your health drains throughout
- Recommended: Have Quick Revive and/or Jug equipped
- Not required, but makes it easier

## Tree Fruit Collection

- Round 8+ required to interact with tree
- Tree has fruit inside (interact to collect)
- Three collection points in forest (auto-teleports between)
- Teleport locations change direction to look, but item locations stay same
- Tip: Hold A+direction, interact, or move+hold to skip animations
There'll be three spots where you can grab the three parts that you have to grab:
1. By what looks like a cabin on the floor to the left of the body. You'll melee the floorboard, and a cutscene will start. You'll find the first part there.
2. In the middle of the forest, on that same rock that the ammo crate is attached to. You will interact with the body. You'll find the second part there.
3. If you go to the back of the forest, you will find the third part. You'll find a body on the ground. That'll be where the third part is.

## Get Wonder Weapon

1. After collecting third part, teleported back to tree
2. Go to Dravakar Sanctuary (where you flipped switches)
3. Use Void Claw to spring-launch yourself up
4. Reach Dravakar's Forge
5. Interact with forge
6. Forge crafts your wonder weapon 
Before you go into the forge, you will want to equip shatter blast on your gun because there will be four shields up there that you'll need to potentially get a shatter blast activation on:
- One will reveal a hammer. That's the part that you need.
- Another one will reveal a lightman ammo mod.
- The other one will reveal a shadow rift ammo mod.
- The other one will reveal a self-revive.

## Weapon Rarity Exchange (Every Round)

Go to Dravakar's Forge and:
1. Take any weapon and place it on the forge
2. Dravakar removes weapon rarity
3. Ether tool drops on ground
4. If weapon has Pack-a-Punch level, corresponding crystal drops

## House Symbols & Exfil Step

**Collecting Symbols:**
- After each basketball shot from house, a symbol appears
- One symbol spawns per round
- Pay attention to order symbols appear

**Exfil Round:**
1. Enter an Exfil round
2. Shoot house symbols in order they appeared
3. Enter Exfil booth
4. Kill spawned HVT
5. Go through Exfil booth → returns to house
6. Girls playing on ground (interact for free Pack-a-Punch crystal)

## Eye Collection & House Lockdown

1. Go upstairs to back of house
2. Charge shot of wonder weapon into toilet
3. Eye of the Forge emerges
4. **Lockdown:** Kill all Doppelghasts and Ravagers
5. Receive phone call → teleporter opens back to map

## Upgrade to Void Talon

1. Interact with Void Gun pedestal
2. Void Gun upgrades to Void Talon
3. Allows double duration usage

## Clear Pillar Walls

**Top of Forge:**
1. Look for three pillars
2. Remove eye squares using Void Gun
3. Go downstairs and remove squares from wall
4. Reveals back of forge
5. Spiders spawn
6. Use charge shot or grenade to break spider webs
7. Repeat on both sides

## Collect Temple Items

**Dravakar's Hammer:**
- Already grabbed from his temple fracture area

**Caltheris's Thread (Shimmering Thread):**
- Use Void Gun to pull orbs toward you
- Interact with orbs
- Eventually drops thread
- Alternative: Go to lantern in Caltheris Sanctuary, interact with orbs as they spawn

**Veytharion's Woven Sash:**
- **Only available when raining**
- Three possible locations:
  - By Jug
  - By PhD
  - By Widow's Wine

**Nixara's Scroll:**
1. Equip Shadow Rift on gun
2. Go to forest
3. Train zombies
4. Get Shadow Rift activation on multiple zombies
5. Eventually scroll drops

## Temple Cleansing Process (All 4 Temples)

**For Each Temple:**
1. Place collected item in temple brazier
2. Pull orb to center of Nexus Forge
3. Turn forge wheel
4. Two purple flames appear in brazier
5. Take flames and light brazier in respective temple area
6. Bring zombie over → becomes infected
7. Take infected to trap (see routing below)

**Trap Routing:**
- Dravakar or Caltheris → Caltheris trap
- Veytharion or Nixara → Veytharion trap
- Bring zombie to middle of trap (auto-routes correctly)
- Activate trap
- Shackle/cuff removes from Titan

**Temple-Specific Plate Mechanics:**

*Caltheris's Temple:*
1. Interact with platform/pedestal
2. ADS gun continuously
3. Platform moves, reveals ocular circle
4. Align circle with left wall
5. Kill zombies → souls crack wall
6. Shoot boss's face (Caltheris)
7. Four soul beings spawn → kill all
8. Shoot face again

*Dravakar's Temple:*
- Same as Caltheris but interact with plate, then stand on it while shooting

*Veytharion's Temple:*
- Same process but jump repeatedly on plate while shooting

*Nixara's Temple:*
- Same process but go prone on plate while aligning lasers

## Final Boss Prep & Forehead Lightning Strikes

**Puzzle for Rain Activation (Veytharion):**
1. Open first door in Veytharion's temple
2. Look left for block puzzle
3. Four blocks around Veytharion temple area
4. Stack order (bottom to top): Water → Plant → Hand → Fire
5. Water on bottom, fire only touches hand
6. Interact with fountain → rain on next round (repeatable each round)

**Boss Lightning Strikes:**
It must be raining. 
1. Turn forge wheel and run downstairs
2. Turn ground dial to enable pillar movement (time-based, move quickly)
3. During rain round, grab purple flame
4. Hit boss in forehead with flame → lightning strike
5. Repeat for all four bosses

**Flexible Sequence:**
- Pillars don't need to be aligned for lightning strikes it just has be raining
- Can do temple lockdowns, pillar alignment, and other bosses simultaneously
- Rotate through all four temples

## Start Boss Fight

1. Go below Nexus Forge
2. Four platforms available (can stand on any)
3. In co-op: all players must stand on platforms to activate
4. Boss fight begins
